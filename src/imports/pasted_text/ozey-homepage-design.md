About Ozey

Ozey is building the operating system for micro & small businesses.

Not another business software.

Not another productivity tool.

It is invisible infrastructure that quietly helps businesses operate.

The philosophy of Ozey is:

Origin → Connection → Structure → Direction

Inspired by how the universe naturally organizes itself.

The inspiration comes from invisible forces, orbital movement, gravitational paths, emergence and connected systems—not from stars, galaxies or science-fiction visuals.

Living Infrastructure

The entire homepage is built on one continuous animated visual system.

Do NOT use straight lines.

Do NOT use sharp angles.

Do NOT use circuit-board graphics.

Instead create one elegant system of huge flowing orbital curves.

Imagine invisible gravitational paths connecting businesses.

The curves should:

span across the entire page
be extremely subtle
use smooth Bézier curves
intersect naturally
fade into darkness
never look decorative

They should feel like hidden infrastructure.

Nodes

Small circular nodes sit along the curves.

These are not stars.

These represent businesses.

Some remain inactive.

Occasionally a node activates.

A tiny blue pulse travels between nodes.

The animation should be extremely slow and elegant.
