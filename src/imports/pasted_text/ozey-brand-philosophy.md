Brand

OZEY

A business operating system for micro & small businesses.

Philosophy

OZEY is invisible infrastructure.

Businesses don't remember operating systems.

They simply rely on them.

OZEY should feel exactly like that.

Quiet.

Confident.

Always working beneath the surface.

Never loud.

Never flashy.

Emotional Goal

The visitor should feel:

"This company thinks differently."

Not

"This is another SaaS company."

The experience should feel calm, intelligent and memorable.

Less marketing.

More philosophy.

Visual Metaphor

Do NOT use abstract blobs.

Do NOT use floating gradients.

Do NOT use generic particles.

Do NOT use AI-style glowing effects.

Instead imagine:

Invisible systems.

Infrastructure.

Navigation.

Signal flow.

Connected pathways.

Energy moving through a network.

The website should feel like watching the hidden operating system behind thousands of businesses quietly come alive.

Visual Identity

Background:

Deep architectural black

#0B0F14

Not pure black.

Typography:

Warm white

#F5F5F2

Accent:

Ozey Blue

#1868DB

Blue must feel precious.

Use it rarely.

Only for:

• interaction
• realization
• active systems
• movement

Never decorate with blue.

Design Language

Extremely minimal.

Large typography.

Large spacing.

Very few elements.

Every pixel should feel intentional.

Imagine Dieter Rams designing software.

Motion Language

The motion system should become Ozey's identity.

Do NOT animate everything.

Animate meaning.

As users scroll...

small invisible routes illuminate.

Tiny signals travel through the page.

Connections become active.

Energy flows.

Then disappears.

It should feel like electricity travelling beneath a city.

Visitors shouldn't consciously notice it.

They should simply feel:

"This website is alive."

Logo Animation

The Ozey logo should never spin.

Instead:

The blue slash slowly energizes.

The slash becomes the source of movement.

One illuminated route leaves the logo.

That route quietly continues throughout the website.

The logo becomes the origin of every interaction.

Typography Animation

Text should never simply fade.

Reveal text diagonally.

Following the angle of the Ozey slash.

Motion should reinforce the logo geometry.

Graphic Language

Build a completely new graphic system.

Inspired by:

Engineering diagrams

Navigation maps

Infrastructure blueprints

Signal routing

Constellation mathematics

Network architecture

Circuit diagrams

NOT astronomy.

NOT galaxies.

NOT stars.

NOT sci-fi.

These graphics should be almost invisible.

Thin.

Elegant.

Purposeful.

They exist only to support the storytelling.

Absolute Rules
Do not imitate CRED.
Do not imitate Apple.
Do not imitate Linear.
Do not imitate Stripe.
Do not use glassmorphism.
Do not use neon cyberpunk.
Do not use generic SaaS illustrations.
Do not use decorative gradients.
Do not use stock business photos.
Do not fill empty space just because it exists.
Every visual element must reinforce the idea of Origin → Connection → Structure → Direction.
The identity should be recognizable even if the logo is removed.
